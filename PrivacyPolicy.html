Song Announcer Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Overview

Song Announcer does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

Song Announcer does not use user accounts, analytics, tracking technologies, or advertising services. No personal information is collected or stored.

Purchases

In-app purchases and free trials are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. Song Announcer does not access, receive, or store any payment information or Apple ID data.

Third-Party Services

Song Announcer does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the support email address provided in the App Store listing.
